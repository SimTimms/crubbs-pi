About

Pilot a spaceship through a scaled solar system. Navigate orbital mechanics to reach distant planets, dock at stations to trade and refuel, manage your fuel, oxygen, and hull integrity, and complete missions that shape the narrative. Every manoeuvre matters — momentum is real, gravity is unforgiving, and space does not forgive carelessness.

Features

Orbital Mechanics

Realistic gravity simulation with sphere-of-influence transitions, circularisation burns, hyperbolic capture, and autopilot-assisted orbit insertion. Plan your burns or let the flight computer handle it.

Docking & Stations

Approach space stations and fuel depots, request docking clearance, and dock to trade cargo, refuel, repair your hull, and pick up new mission contracts.

Combat & Scanning

Railguns and laser systems for ship-to-ship combat. Magnetic, drive signature, and radio scanners to detect threats and identify targets before they detect you.

Resource Management

Monitor fuel reserves, oxygen levels, hull integrity, and power distribution. Every thruster burn costs fuel, every second drains O2, and collisions compromise your hull.

NPC & Narrative

Dialogue trees with NPC crews, branching story missions delivered through an in-game inbox, radio chatter from passing ships, and faction reputation that shapes your journey.

Solar System

A scaled solar system with orbiting planets, an asteroid belt, nebula clouds, and a procedural star field. Each body exerts gravitational influence on your trajectory.
